Extract this bundle into the root of the aarav-hyprland repository.

Example:

  cd ~/Projects/aarav-hyprland
  tar -xzf /path/to/aarav-hyprland-installer-bundle.tar.gz --strip-components=1

Then validate:

  bash -n install.sh doctor.sh restore.sh uninstall.sh
  python -m py_compile tools/state-manager.py
  ./install.sh --dry-run
